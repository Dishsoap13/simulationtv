Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

SimulationTV ("we") runs a private tool that posts our own simulation videos to our own TikTok account. This policy explains what that tool does with data.

What we collect

When our account owner connects TikTok, TikTok gives the tool an access token and basic profile information (display name, username and open ID). We use it only to show who is posting and to upload our own videos.

What we don't do

We don't collect data from viewers or other TikTok users. We don't sell, share or rent any data. We don't use data for advertising.

Where it's stored

Tokens are stored only on our own computer and are never sent anywhere except TikTok's official API. Disconnecting in the tool, or removing the app in TikTok's settings, deletes access.
